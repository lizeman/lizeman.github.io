Playground

Some small interactive things I’ve built into this site for fun.

⌨︎ Typing Trial Three lengths × three categories, blinking caret, personal-best tracking, last-5-runs sparkline, and a per-key error report. ⚙ Busy Beaver BB(2)/BB(3)/BB(4) champions on an animated tape with heat-fading cells, a state-diagram side panel, trajectory mini-map, and step-back history. ⊙ Roulette European single-zero wheel with chip-stack betting and a slow-spinning canvas. Five betting strategies — Martingale, Fibonacci, D’Alembert, Labouchère, flat — plus a simulator with bankroll sparkline and persistent peak-profit leaderboard. 𓃴 Buffalo Watch the famous one-word English sentence grow longer and longer — every "buffalo" colored by its role (city, animal, or verb) with a parse tree. ✦ Portal Three short riddles. Solve them and a hidden room of toys opens up.
