About

Hi! My name is Zeman Li (李泽慢). I am currently a student researcher at Google Research and a third-year Ph.D. student in the Industrial and Systems Engineering Department at the University of Southern California. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Meisam Razaviyayn.

I received my first B.S. in Applied Mathematics at Emory University and my second B.S. in Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology. During my time at Georgia Tech, I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Xie Yao.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of large-scale optimization and machine learning, with a focus on developing data-efficient methods for training foundation models, as well as privacy-preserving machine learning.
